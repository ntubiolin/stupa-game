# Nepal–Tibet environment references

The world is scaled for a pilgrimage on foot. The Unreal origin is the approach south of each primary landmark. Positive X points north, positive Y points east and one metre is 100 Unreal units. Each site has a fresh walkable mesh. Buildings use original geometry. Field photographs and plans informed massing, sightlines, colour and the relationship between buildings and terrain.

| Stage | Reference and modelling interpretation | Comparison view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boudhanath | The official site plan places an open circumambulation path between the stupa precinct and its enclosing buildings. Cross terraces support the dome, four pairs of eyes and the thirteen-ring spire. [UNESCO site plan](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/121/maps/) and [entrance and ambulatory photographs](https://whc.unesco.org/document/187827) | Approach facing the dome and flanking buildings |
| Grove | The game's votive grove has eight small chortens around a central shrine. Small votive stupas and prayer wheels at the real Boudhanath site informed the vocabulary. [Monument inventory and photographs](https://whc.unesco.org/document/187827) | Central shrine and ring of votives |
| Wilderness | Original alpine approach. The wooded southern slopes and rock passes of the Himalayan pilgrimage routes informed the layered terrain. [Nekhor, Helambu](https://www.nekhor.org/padmasambhava/beyul/helambu) | Trail foreground, cairns, distant snowline |
| Pharping | The upper Asura cave is above the town in forest. Lower Yangleshö is next to a monastery. This level compresses the approach into steps, a cave facade and a neighbouring monastery. [Nekhor photos and separate site maps](https://www.nekhor.org/padmasambhava/nepal/asura-yanglesho) | Rock face rising behind monastery roofs |
| Mangyul | Woodland transitions to a pair of alpine ridgelines. A pass camp and prayer flags mark the trail. [Gyirong field photographs](https://english.news.cn/20230329/57034c8a2bf7417dbf07648aadb71fad/c.html) and [regional route map](https://treasuryoflives.org/map/download?file=main_map-sm.pdf&small=1) | Flags near the saddle, mountain layers beyond |
| Yarlung | A narrow, tall tower and the adjoining lower palace share a spur above valley cultivation. The bridge and switchback steps shorten the playable approach. [Yumbu Lagang map](https://treasuryoflives.org/institution/Yumbu-Lagang) and [Shannan photographs](https://lyfzj.shannan.gov.cn/zjsn/jdjs/202603/t20260310_165492.html) | Valley floor looking up to the stepped palace silhouette |
| Samye | Utse forms the centre of the mandala plan, surrounded by satellites, four coloured stupas and a low enclosing wall. [Nekhor plan and field photographs](https://www.nekhor.org/padmasambhava/tibet/samye-monastery) | Southern axis through the courtyard to the main temple |
| Chimphu | Cave refuges and modest retreat buildings occupy multiple hillside elevations above Samye. [Treasury of Lives location and archival photograph](https://treasuryoflives.org/en/institution/Samye-Chimpu) and [Nekhor site survey](https://www.nekhor.org/padmasambhava/tibet/samye-chimpu) | Ascending stair with dwellings set between outcrops |
| Terma I / II / III | Original underground turquoise deposit, glacial crystal sanctuary and golden mandala vault from the existing dungeon themes. [Rubin Museum, mandala form and three-dimensional representations](https://rubinmuseum.org/projecthimalayanart/exhibition/living-practices/religious-goals/mandala-of-chakrasamvara/) | Compare each floor along its entrance axis toward the mineral deposit or raised shrine |

A dated [1963 photograph of freshly renewed saffron markings](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boudhanath_Stupa_fresh_paint,_Kathmandu,_Nepal_1963.jpg) informed the broad petal strokes across the whitewashed dome. The harmika uses gilded faces, painted eyes and a square stepped spire.

The geographic references describe modern or documented historic sites. The nine playable maps include interpretive landscapes, and the three dungeon floors are separate cooked maps. Built-in procedural colour and relief tiles provide matching materials when shared scan overrides are not assigned. No field photographs are bundled.
